About

I'm currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing and a Faculty Fellow at the Institute for Artificial Intelligence at the University of Georgia. I'm also a Lilly Teaching Fellow.

Professional Bio

Dr. Jin Sun is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing and a Faculty Fellow at the Institute for Artificial Intelligence at the University of Georgia. He is also a Lilly Teaching Fellow. His main research area is computer vision—understanding the world using visual data with the help of state-of-the-art deep learning models. In particular, he is passionate about understanding objects in diverse and complex environments using images and videos. He is actively collaborating with researchers to apply AI in interdisciplinary research to push the boundaries of scientific discovery and improve people's quality of life. His work has been published in top computer vision and machine learning conferences such as CVPR, ECCV, ICCV, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR. His research work has been selected as Notable Books and Articles in ACM Computing Reviews in 2014 and Best Paper Nominee in CVPR 2020.
